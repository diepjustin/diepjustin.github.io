About Me

I'm a senior journalism student at the University of Nebraska-Lincoln and news intern at the Lincoln Journal Star.

I serve as an assistant news editor and photographer at The Daily Nebraskan. I lead coverage of the NU Board of Regents and university administration, including breaking stories on UNL's budget cuts in 2025 using leaked internal documents. As a photographer, I've covered spot news, political rallies, concerts and Husker Athletics including Nebraska Volleyball at the semifinals at the 2024 NCAA Tournament.

I also serve as a videographer for Jacht, a student-run advertising agency, producing brand storytelling and marketing content for clients.

My earlier experience includes internships at the St. Louis Post-Dispatch, where I reported on the aftermath of a jail riot; the Omaha World-Herald, where I collaborated on coverage of an ICE raid at an Omaha meatpacking plant and its aftermath; and Enterprise Media Group, where I was selected for UNL's Rural Nebraska Journalism Program to report across eastern Nebraska and western Iowa. I previously was a marketing intern at E & A Consulting Group, a civil engineering firm in Omaha.

I got my start in journalism at Omaha Bryan High School, first taking photos for The Crusader yearbook and later reporting, editing and designing The Orator newspaper. I was named the 2023 Nebraska High School Journalist of the Year during my senior year.

Run-Hide-Fight Active Shooter Preparedness

I directed this educational video for the University of Nebraska-Lincoln Police Department as part of a class project in the Production House experience lab. The video promotes campus safety by detailing the "Run, Hide, Fight" active shooter response strategy.

On the Border: Sherry Wounded Foot's Story

I assisted with filming an in-depth investigative reporting documentary led by Marissa Lindemann and Olivia Reed, and published by Nebraska News Service. Lindemann and Reed created the documentary for an in-depth reporting class on murdered and missing marginalized women. Specifically, I was brought on to help capture interviews and b-roll during their trip to Pine Ridge, South Dakota.

*The documentary received an Award of Excellence from the Broadcast Education Association On Location.
